Projects and Extracurricular Activities

Mototech Association, ESTACA

AMIE-E Project

Turning a production electric motorcycle, a Zero SR, into a competition machine for the French "Montée Impossible" (extreme hill climb) championship. The project adapts the motorcycle to this discipline by redesigning its chassis so that it withstands the demands of a tough race.

  • Mechanical design as a team: lengthened swingarm, custom-made
  • Selection of a Duke 990 frame to house all the components of the electric motorcycle
  • Modelling and simulation in 3DEXPERIENCE to check the frame's resistance to loads
Company: Lider

Study of a trailer to propose technical improvements and new design solutions, as part of a team of engineering students.

  • Cost study carried out in parallel to check the feasibility of the proposed solutions
